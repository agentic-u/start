# Recover worthwhile opportunities

This is a draft method to exercise with a principal, not a claim of qualified sales expertise. The principal's request supplies the work; this package supplies a method. It grants no access or permission. The repository's BOOTSTRAP.md routes work here; do not restart onboarding or install the AgenticU runtime for this skill.

Read [the requirements](references/requirements.md) and [the local fitting protocol](references/fitting.md). Use their scoped branches; missing send access must not prevent useful read-only investigation. Use the person's established business workspace and native tools. Ask for the workspace only if it cannot be established safely; do not create a second business or repeat a general intake.

## Aim and standard

Help suitable unfinished opportunities reach justified next outcomes, including releasing unsuitable work, while leaving follow-up intelligible and owned. Do not optimize reply counts, calendar volume or wins independently of fit, capacity and economics. An estimate is not cash; apparent interest is not a sale. Unknown status is not lost or open.

## Fit the method here

Discover existing source locations, tool/account identities, working records and standing instructions. Inspect the minimum evidence needed to establish their role and current usability. Use the local lock protocol to select implementations and retain unresolved needs. Reuse existing terminology and status meanings; keep a private local interpretation where mapping is necessary. Do not migrate their business into our preferred CRM or require a new platform for the exercise.

If no adequate implementation is available, use a known procedure for that specific need when available; otherwise describe the exact missing ability and a concrete option. Install, connect or change tools within actual authority. Ask only for the owner's unavailable action, decision or fact; continue independent branches and state their limits.

## Work the opportunities

1. Establish the principal's immediate aim, available capacity, relevant boundary conditions and scope of authority from the request and available records. Ask about material gaps. A schedule opening is a reason to investigate, not a mandate to fill it at any price.
2. Reconcile estimates, proposal versions, conversation chronology, bookings and payment evidence where relevant. Identify customers/projects by actual source identity. Retain discrepancies and source references. Do not overwrite one system's status merely because another appears newer; establish what changed.
3. For each candidate, judge whether another conversation is worthwhile, what is known, which question remains and who should act. Separate a customer decision from our missing follow-up. Stop chasing a clear decline; do not contact someone as undecided when the records already establish acceptance or payment.
4. When value or scope is unclear, use the actual offered work, genuine proof and homeowner priorities to improve the explanation. Preserve original proposals. Do not invent differentiation, competitor scope, warranties, urgency or promises. Changes to price, scope or dates require the appropriate business decision.
5. Choose a useful next action: resolve an internal fact, prepare or conduct a specific conversation, revise a proposal, arrange an appropriate visit, wait for a dependency, or release the opportunity. Prioritize business value and timing rather than blindly sending to every stale record. No mandatory numerical score is needed.
6. Execute through the selected tools within authority. Before consequential commitments, check the relevant current facts. After writes, inspect the actual result. If delivery is unknown, reconcile using the tool's record/history before retrying. A draft, a sent message and a delivered message are different observations.
7. Interpret replies in context. Update the actual working record, carry questions and exceptions to the appropriate person, and change course when the business changes. Do not treat objections as permission to pressure a homeowner or promise unapproved concessions.

## Carry the responsibility

Keep every active case's source references, current conclusion, last verified action, next action/trigger, owner and pending decision in the existing working system. Use local notes only for information the native system cannot adequately retain; link them instead of maintaining a second contradictory pipeline.

For recurrence, use a supported event or scheduling facility only when needed and authorized, and verify it was actually registered. If no facility exists, report that work waits for an explicit return; do not promise to watch an inbox from a completed chat.

At handover leave a compact entry pointing to the selected method/version, local lock, active cases, waits and exceptions. Keep one clearly current summary: replace superseded availability, decisions and next actions in that summary, retaining earlier states in the normal history instead of appending a correction below an obsolete headline. A fresh agent verifies relevant current access/authority, reads changed source records and continues rather than repeating intake. Reconcile the handover against those current records before relying on it. Write only what the next worker needs; do not replace original source with a summary.

## Review the result

Show the principal what moved, what was deliberately not pursued, why, and what needs their judgment. Cite the actual outputs and action records. Distinguish preparation, external action, customer response, booked/sold work, fulfilled work and collected money. Leave unobserved stages unknown. Identify meaningful method/tool gaps for improvement; keep client facts private. A correct decision not to pursue can be a useful outcome.
