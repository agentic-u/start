# Launch a measured commercial journey

Experimental source-adapted method, not a promise of demand or conversion. Start through BOOTSTRAP.md with the owner's actual request and existing business. Keep the whole business and downstream delivery in view; use build-business when that larger responsibility is yours. No generic onboarding or platform installation is a prerequisite.

## Recover the business and choose the intervention

Read original customer/expertise material, current offer and terms, real proof, existing acquisition, economics and delivery capacity. Establish the owner's aim and the important current constraint. Distinguish observations, source theory, model hypotheses and unresolved decisions. Do not invent buyer language, testimonials, results, approvals or offer terms. A prior business's campaign is a donor, not this business's strategy.

Recommend a useful intervention from those facts. Psychology belongs in understanding what the person already wants, believes, fears, tried and needs to believe next; a rubric score is not conversion evidence. TOC helps choose what to change and what would disprove the constraint hypothesis; do not make a compulsory tree or arbitrary layer limit before doing work. Take owner-only commercial choices to the principal with concrete options while independent preparation continues.

## Make the actual offer and persuasive work

Keep one current offer/terms source across page, creative, checkout or booking, follow-up and fulfilment. Include scope, price/currency if relevant, conditions, remedy, capacity, actual proof and next step. Preserve historical promises. Do not silently turn a lab case or modeled sale into public customer proof.

Read the actual selected reference/control and customer material before adapting it. Identify what makes the buyer notice, understand, believe, want and act, including smaller nested jobs and objections. Select the target argument before prose; preserve selling force and concrete desired outcomes. Rebuild mechanisms and proof for this offer rather than swapping nouns. Keep uncertain factual/release notes outside customer copy. Write strong complete copy and produce actual needed creative through the chosen model/tools; obey the principal's model and review choices. Do not replace persuasive writing with an internal evaluation checklist.

Counterread the target alone for selling force and against its original source/selected direction. Retain rejected drafts, exact feedback, corrections, source and asset versions. A script, outline, manifest or a seller's self-score is not finished media or principal acceptance.

## Build the whole journey

Choose the implementation from the job and local fitting, not a mandatory framework. Build the appropriate destination and fulfilment, then connect the public path: page/creative → action → intake/qualification → booking/checkout or equivalent → confirmation → useful delivery → follow-up/review. Avoid an empty lead form whose submissions go nowhere. Persist actual records and give staff a usable return path. Respect consent and communication authority; a local spool is not an external send.

Compose [measure-commercial-journey](../measure-commercial-journey/SKILL.md) while building, before traffic. Keep the same offer, audience, identity and event meaning coherent across components. Fit required hosting, domain, forms/storage, scheduling/payment/delivery and analytics through the capability lock. State actual missing means and continue useful work; ask for owner-only setup only when available authorised access cannot resolve it. Never copy sign-in state or reset the owner's environment.

When administration must stay with the owner, use a concrete actuation request: selected account/project, new versus existing resources, exact permitted operations, immutable release archive and file hashes, configuration overlay, expected native receipts and subsequent query/update responsibility. Verify that the requested resource names are unused before creation. Preserve old bundles; do not silently deploy a stale archive after source or namespace changes. Return actual resource IDs and readbacks without moving administrative credentials into the agent's environment. Record owner assistance explicitly; a proposed bridge is not a callable capability. A provider quota rejection is a real missing means: preserve it, leave billing and unrelated resources alone, continue independent work and take any changed target or plan to its owner.

Deploy the actual build to the authorised target. Verify reachable exact bytes/version, desktop and mobile journey, validation/failure states, persisted intake, destination, consent/identity, tracking and downstream handoff. Use marked test submissions/transactions within authority and read effects back. A build, public URL or analytics hit is not complete delivery. An actual deployed lab preview remains distinct from real commercial release and demand.

## Acquire traffic and improve the business

Prepare the selected campaign with concrete audience/hypothesis, actual creative, destination, budget, decision rules and operational owner. Fit and verify the actual platform/account when platform operation is claimed. Draft/paused objects, mocked APIs and copied campaign settings do not qualify live ads. Do not spend or activate merely because assets exist; use the specific authority granted for that campaign.

Read real or explicitly modeled results at their native source and clock. Reconcile qualified responses, sales/money/refunds, fulfilment and capacity, not clicks alone. Distinguish broken measurement from weak demand or a delivery constraint. Choose the next bounded causal hypothesis, make the actual change, preserve version/release/response, and revise the business understanding. Attribution is not automatically causal lift.

Leave current sources, offer, deployed journey, creative, event law, native records, actual tests, releases, failures/assistance and next responsibility intelligible to a fresh operator. Preserve full activity and original outputs outside temporary tools before cleanup. A successful case is evidence about that case, not universal expertise.
