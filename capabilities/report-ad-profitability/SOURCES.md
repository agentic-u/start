# Why this method has this shape

This is an adaptation of an operating commerce reporting method. Source business
records, identifiers, rates, account configuration and credentials are deliberately
absent. These are source pins and behavioral lineage, not a portable copy of the
private reporting installation or a claim of current financial results.

public instructions paraphrase them without copying private receipts or business

separating dated use from current access and qualification.
