# Build and operate the business

Experimental method, not qualified business expertise or a promise of commercial success. The principal supplies the aim and authority. Read BOOTSTRAP.md's actual-request routing, preserve their existing workspace and follow the current request. Do not install the current AU runtime or repeat generic onboarding as a prerequisite.

## Start with people and work already here

Read original expertise, resources, constraints and available customer conversations. If a customer is present, engage now within authority. Ask a useful question or make a useful offer while independent investigation continues. Do not spend days constructing an empty company before talking to a person. Interest, willingness to try and a purchase are different facts.

Do not presume a course, agency, subscription or software business. Establish what valuable work the principal can deliver, who needs it, alternatives, and fit with the principal's aims. Keep assumptions and disagreement visible. A failed hypothesis changes the work.

## Keep the whole enterprise in view

Retain one current business understanding covering principal/resources; customer/market; model/economics; offer/terms; actual product/service; identity/proof; acquisition; selling; transaction/agreement; onboarding; fulfilment/quality; support/refunds; outcomes/retention; finance; people/capacity; relevant obligations; infrastructure/access; continued operation; management/improvement; recovery/handover. An inapplicable branch needs its reason; an unresolved branch needs an owner/next step. Headings are not completion.

Build concrete useful paths. A customer's question becomes a decision, offer, agreement/payment if accepted, intake, delivered work, review/support and financial record. Price, promises, scope, capacity and instructions must agree. Customers must receive/use what was sold. Reuse suitable tools; build missing means cleanly when the job needs them. Existing SDKs are optional precedents, not mandatory dependencies.

Take consequential source/business changes through affected work. Preserve historical commitments rather than applying new terms silently. A refund, delayed input or capacity change can affect delivery, finance, communication and acquisition together.

## Fit capability here

Keep private capability.lock.md or an adequate existing equivalent: actual method source/commit and consulted file hashes; abilities required by active work; selected tool/account/workspace identities; observed usable, failed, unknown or waiting states; authority/evidence references; unresolved dependencies and next actions. Bound means selected, not proven ready. No credentials.

For each actual dependency establish the information/action needed and whether the chosen implementation provides it here. Reading/drafting/sending, payment recording/collection, preparation/deployment and source availability/permission are distinct. Resolve progressively; continue independent work. Ask the principal only for missing decisions, grants or facts requiring them. A method/connection cannot grant authority.

## Make and operate actual things

Make usable media, pages, documents, tools and services through ordinary tools. Inspect results and judge quality, not existence alone. Connect native event/record owners and read back effects. Reconcile uncertain sends/payments/deployments before retrying. Never invent customers, transactions, testimonials, credentials or provider success. Separate simulation/provider test mode from real commercial outcomes.

Maintain each customer's current scope/terms, source inputs, responsibility, verified last action, next trigger and unresolved choices. Reconcile finance against actual recorded transactions, fees, refunds and obligations; forecasts differ from observed money. Required professional/provider/human decisions retain their actual status.

When ongoing work is requested and authorized, register a supported trigger and verify its receipt. A written task is not a process; an idle conversation is not monitoring. Retain a usable return path if operation isn't activated. Keep failures visible and bounded by authority.

## Continue and improve

Leave one clearly current handover linking original sources, business understanding, fitting, customer work, money, triggers and decisions. Replace stale conclusions in that summary; retain history separately. Fresh agents check current facts and continue without duplicate effects or repeated intake.

Extract reusable methods/implementations where concrete work demonstrates need. Separate their contract from private facts, accounts and permissions. A second situation or receiving business tests whether a part travels. Preserve failures/assistance; a lucky outcome is not universal expertise.

Report what exists, what happened, what remains unobserved and what needs the principal. A marketing plan, website or set of files alone is not an operating business. Useful work and an honest current state take precedence over declaring success.
