# Your agents, working in your business

Start in T3 Code with your ChatGPT plan connected. Send:

> Read https://github.com/agentic-u/start and set me up.

Your agent starts with what you already have. If you need setup, it prepares a business folder, opens it as a T3 project and helps you understand what to work on first. If you already have a business project and a job in mind, give it this repository and describe the job. Your words and work stay on your computer.

**For the agent:** read [BOOTSTRAP.md](BOOTSTRAP.md) in full. Follow the person's actual request and existing state: resume, use a relevant [capability](CAPABILITIES.md), or prepare setup when needed. Use the repository's instructions and companion files; record the commit followed. Do not require the AgenticU runtime/CLI or repeat onboarding to do business work.

This is an early Mac-first release under test. The setup includes local Git, Codex defaults, T3 project configuration and OpenCode Browse. Browser extension installation/pairing and account sign-ins can need the person's involvement; continue independent business work while a connection is waiting. Installing an extension is not proof that browser control works.

Setup sends no business messages, spends no money and publishes no business files. Later business work uses only the authority the person actually gives. This repository is public; their business records stay separate and are not published by following it.
